  • reviewing feedback the hurd pr had received.
  • reviewing new feedback from yesterday on the nuttx pr.

the first task was fairly straightforward. i seemed to have gotten the upstream source wrong. the one i was cross-referencing hadn’t been updated since 2021. i had just assumed this was expected, considering the gnu hurd site and docs themselves are a bit outdated. but apparently they transitioned to using upstream glibc. so i went through the patches again and made some tweaks to adapt it to upstream glibc. this resulted in proper handling of time_t values and of the glob and globfree routines. these were redirected to different routines when the time bits feature test macro was issued. that was not part of the legacy glibc fork i had initially based my work off of. there were also some other minor modifications.
